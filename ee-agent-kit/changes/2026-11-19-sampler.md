### Fixed
- `ee-agent-kit/lead/gate-and-push.sh` no longer leaves its sampler running, or its sleep holding the gate's output, when the gate ends: the sampler sleeps on a pipe that ends with the gate, whatever ends it, SIGKILL to the gate or its group included (the health gate of 2026-11-19 waited 60 s). On a stop the lock goes only once make and its whole process group have ended (SIGKILL after GATE_STOP_SECONDS, default 30), also when GNU make waits for a test that ignores SIGTERM, where the gate used to hang for good. 2 signals at once, a caller's closed pipe (now exit 141), a signal just after the lock, or a signal to the gate's process group while it writes the lock no longer leave the lock behind for good. A hung quiet look no longer holds off make's stop, and it is killed with the sampler; a failed look is written to the .foreign file. GATE_STOP_SECONDS, GATE_SAMPLE_SECONDS, GATE_QUIET_SECONDS, GATE_QUIET_TRIES and GATE_QUIET_LIMIT are checked (exit 2); a fraction of GATE_QUIET_SECONDS, or a GATE_QUIET_TRIES of 0, made the gate push while helpers ran. A Ctrl-C before the lock is a stop (130), not 'HEAD is not <commit>'. A signal as make starts no longer leaves the gate waiting for good with the lock held.
