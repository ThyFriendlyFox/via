### Changed
- A bounded download of install.sh is watched pass by pass. A pass whose rate over its last 120 s (`APT_WATCH_WINDOW`), less its longest still stretch up to 60 s, would not fetch the rest of what apt said it needs within what is left of `APT_DOWNLOAD_TIMEOUT` is ended, and the next pass follows after the wait. CI then puts the host that served it last in the mirror list (`APT_SLOW_PASS_COMMAND`, `tools/ci/apt_archive.sh --slow`). In CI run 44506767788 (2026-11-19) the first host sent a 1 MiB sample at 2.47 MB/s and the download at 48.3 kB/s, and 1 pass used all 570 s. The pass's line and every stop of a bounded download name the hosts asked, the bytes that arrived of the bytes apt named, and the rate, where the stop said `apt said: nothing`. apt's `Need to get` line is shown. A person's computer sets no bound and is not watched. The ranking step's name and lines say it ranks by a 1 MiB sample.
