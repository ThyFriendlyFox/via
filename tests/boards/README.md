# Fixture boards

These projects are the tests of the gates. Each one exists to make 1 gate
behave in a known way.

| Project | Expected | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `pass-rc-divider` | GREEN on `workcenter gates`, with `--strict` too | A 2-resistor divider with an RC filter. Schematic, board, and a sim deck with bounds. Its `sim/corners.json` names -40 and 85 C, 1 % resistors, a 10 % capacitor and 8 Monte Carlo draws: 46 corner runs, the output between 2.4605 and 2.54175 V, 8.25 mV inside its nearer bound. |
| `pass-first-design` | GREEN on `workcenter gates`, with `--strict` too | The first design file, built: the 1 fixture that goes from a design file through all 4 gates. `design.py` is `templates/project/design.py` to the byte. The schematic and the project file are what `workcenter design` writes from it; a test compares. `workcenter design design.py --route` wrote the board, and DRC holds the board to the schematic. On a 5 V supply the deck holds 2 bounds over 46 corner runs. The output stays between 2.4605 and 2.5417 V, and `f3` between 280.2 and 365.5 Hz. `f3` is the frequency where the output is 0.3536 of the input: the low-pass corner at a gain of 0.5. The pictures of the root `README.md` are of this board. |
| `fail-drc-clearance` | ERC pass, DRC fail with a `clearance` violation | The same board with 1 track moved inside the clearance of a pad. |
| `fail-sim-spec` | SIM fail on the `vout` bound | The divider deck with bounds that the circuit cannot meet. No board, no corners: the gate names their absence as a warning. |
| `fail-sim-corner` | SIM fail at a corner, each bound holding as written | 2 decks, no board. `diode_ref`: a diode's forward voltage, 0.548 V at 27 C and 0.42 V at 85 C against a 0.50 to 0.60 V window. `divider`: 2.5 V as written, 2.375 V and 2.625 V with its 5 % resistors apart against 2.45 to 2.55 V. 32 corner runs; each failure line names the worst corner and how many more miss. |
| `pass-lc-filter` | SIM pass with `--strict` | 3 decks, no board: a 15.9 kHz input filter swept with `.ac`, a 100 kHz load clear of it, and a 16 kHz clock on its resonance. `sim/frequency-plan.json` names both sources and the resonance, and answers the clock's overlap with the deck that drives it. 94 corner runs: the deck that answers the overlap is also run with L stepped against C between their ends. |
| `fail-freq-overlap` | SIM fail on the frequency plan alone | The same filter with a load at 5.3 kHz, whose third harmonic is the resonance: the rail swings 6.8 V on a 5 V supply while the deck's one bound, on the average, holds. Only the plan sees it. |

| `pass-board-ecss` | DRC pass with `--strict`: `board rules ecss-q-st-70-12c-rev1: 0 fail, 0 not judged` | The divider on an 80 x 80 mm outline, 1.6 mm thick, with `board-rules.json` naming ECSS-Q-ST-70-12C Rev.1. Its `ratings.json` states VIN 0 to 5.25 V, VOUT 0 to 2.625 V, GND 0 V, and 1 A on J1 pins 1 and 3. Every gap is 5.25 V at most, so 0.25 mm as designed on an outer layer (table 13-7, page 130). VIN and GND are drawn 1.4 mm: 1 A asks 0.752 mm, and VIN beside GND, under 25 mm apart, is 1 conductor of 2 A that asks 0.064379 mm2 as manufactured, where the 2 give 0.0675 mm2 (13.6.3g; `tests/test_boardrules_drc.py` works both out). |
| `fail-board-ecss` | DRC fail on 2 lines of the pack, 0 errors of KiCad's own | The same board with 1 VIN segment left at 0.25 mm, and a GND stub on B.Cu that ends 0.22 mm from J1 pin 1 (VIN). KiCad also warns that the stub has an unconnected end: a warning, which fails only a `--strict` run. |

Its `ratings.json` states the 5 V rail, the signal and the load; `parts.csv` names each part's number. |
| `fail-ratings-pin` | `ratings` fails on 1 line, `pin.voltage` | U3 and its capacitor stand on +5V. Its output puts the rail's 5.25 V on U2's input, which takes 4.6 V. |
| `fail-ratings-load` | `ratings` fails on 1 line, `output.current.linear` | The schematic of `pass-ratings-node`. Its `ratings.json` states 1.9 A for the load: 0.95 of the 2 A the regulator gives, and the pack allows a plastic linear part 0.70. |

| `fail-sim-thermal` | SIM fail on 1 line, `thermal: U1`, at 85 C | 1 deck, no board: a TLV75533PDBVR from 5 V to 3.3 V into a 33 ohm CRCW2512 of 1 % and -100 ppm/K. `sim/thermal.json` names U1 and the ECSS pack: 110 C, the lower of 110 C and 150 C - 40 C. | `fail-sim-bias` | SIM fail on 1 line, the `vpp` bound, which names C2 | The schematic of `fail-ratings-bias` with C2 a 10 uF 10 V Samsung CL10A106KP8NNN, and L1 Coilcraft's XAL4020-222MEC. `sim/buck.cir` is the regulator's power stage at 1.5 MHz into the 1.1 A load. | `pass-fields-microstrip` | `workcenter fields --strict` prints `FIELDS HELD` | A board file alone, written by hand: 1 track 10 mm long and 0.36 mm wide on F.Cu, over a GND pour on B.Cu, 0.2 mm of dielectric of 4.5 between. `fields.json` holds 3 made-up wide limits for 0.1 to 10 GHz, and says so. openEMS 0.0.35 gave 50.88 ohm (the median over 197 frequencies), 25.57 dB of return loss at 10 GHz and 0.16 dB of insertion loss at 5.59 GHz, on 58968 cells in 8.1 s (3770 steps). Hammerstad and Jensen give 51.34 ohm for this line with copper as a sheet: the run is 0.9% under it, and `tests/test_fields_solver.py` holds it within 3%. With the track's edges on mesh lines the run gave 43.14 ohm, 16% under. |
| `fail-fields-stub` | `fields` fails on 1 line, `insertion_loss_db` | The same stack-up, the track cut at its middle by an open stub 7 mm long. The stub is a quarter wave between c / (4 x 7 mm x sqrt(4.5)) = 5.05 GHz and c / (4 x 7 mm) = 10.71 GHz, and shorts the line there: openEMS 0.0.35 gave 30.10 dB at 5.81 GHz against a made-up 3 dB, on 111150 cells in 20.4 s; 2 runs, each of 9405 steps, gave it to the last digit. The net branches at (105, 100), so its impedance is not judged. The ports are named by 2 pads, J2 turned 90 degrees. Without them the stub's open end would be a third port, and the command refuses that. |
| `pass-copper-strip` | `workcenter copper --strict` prints `COPPER HELD` | The board of `copper_strip.py`: J1 feeds 5 V along a 1.5 mm F.Cu track 44 mm to J2, and GND back on B.Cu; R1 (220R) hangs off the supply. `ratings.json` states 1 A at J2 and 0.024 A through R1; `copper.json` names each net's entry on J1 and the fixture's own limits: 50 mV, 50 A/mm2, 105 C. The run: +5V drops 14.64 mV, 1 A in 1.5 mm is 19.05 A/mm2, R1's 0.1267 W puts the board at 60.07 C. |
| `fail-copper-neck` | `copper` fails on 1 line, `+5V current density` | The same board with the supply 0.3 mm wide for 4 mm in its middle: 1 A in 0.3 mm x 0.035 mm is 95.24 A/mm2, over the 50 the fixture allows, and 104.3 where the wide track's round end meets the neck. The drop (18.51 mV) and the heat (60.23 C) hold. |
| `library` | No board: the facts of the ratings fixtures' parts | `library/facts/`, found by `workcenter ratings` as a project's own library is: in a folder above the project. The regulator, the 2 buffers, the resistors, the bypass capacitors and the 2 inductors are real parts, each entry read from its maker's datasheet with the URL, the SHA256 and the pages. The 10 uF capacitors and the headers are made up, and their entries say so. `ti-tlv755p.json` is the thermal fixtures' regulator, with its thermal figures; no second reader has read it yet. |

Symbols and footprints are embedded in the files, so no library tables are
needed to run them. `build/` output is ignored by git.

The 6 ratings fixtures are 1 design, `ratings_node.py`, with 1 fault
planted in each of 5. A fixture's `design.py` names its fault; its
`node.kicad_sch` and `node.kicad_pro` are what `workcenter design` writes
from it, and a test compares. To write them again, and no board with them:

    workcenter design tests/boards/<fixture>/design.py --out <a scratch folder>

and copy the 2 files in. The boards are not routed and not built: `ratings`
reads the schematic alone.

The 2 copper fixtures are 1 design, `copper_strip.py`, with the neck planted in 1. Their tracks are
drawn in the design, not routed; `workcenter design tests/boards/<fixture>/design.py` writes all 3
KiCad files again, and a test compares them.

`kicad-cli` writes a `<name>.kicad_prl` next to the project when it runs.
It is a KiCad side file with no design content and is ignored by git.
